How to play

  1. Steer

    Move your mouse, or drag anywhere on the screen with your finger. Your snake follows the direction you point.

  2. Eat & grow

    Collect glowing orbs to get longer. The longer you are, the higher you climb on the leaderboard.

  3. Boost & trap

    Hold the mouse button, Space or the ⚡ button to boost. Make other snakes crash into your body and eat what they leave behind.

Tips to become the longest snake

  • Circle smaller snakes. Wrap your body around them until they have nowhere to go.
  • Boost only when it pays. It costs length, so use it to escape or to cut someone off.
  • Watch the red wall. Touching the edge of the arena ends your run.
  • Follow the fights. A big snake that dies leaves a trail of orbs. Be the first to arrive.
  • Head-on, size wins. When two heads collide, the bigger snake survives.

Frequently asked questions

Is Snake Arena free?

Yes. Snake Arena is completely free to play in your web browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game runs directly in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. On iPhone and Android, drag your finger anywhere on the screen to steer and hold the ⚡ button to boost.

How do I boost?

On a computer, hold the left mouse button or the Space bar. On mobile, hold the ⚡ button. Boosting makes you faster but burns some of your length.

What happens when my snake dies?

Your snake turns into glowing orbs that other players can eat. Press Play again to respawn instantly.

Are there bots in the arena?

Yes. When few people are online, bots join the arena so there is always someone to chase. They play by the same rules as everyone else, so they can be trapped and eaten too.
